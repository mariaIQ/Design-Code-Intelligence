# Evidence-led Design

Build a visible chain: `evidence → insight → opportunity → design response → test → outcome`.

Distinguish observation, interpretation, assumption and recommendation. Triangulate user needs with operations, policy, technology, accessibility and measurable business outcomes. Choose only artifacts that help a decision; avoid ceremonial process diagrams.

Define what must be learned next, the lightest credible method and the decision it unlocks. End with what is supported, what remains uncertain, what should change and how success will be measured.
