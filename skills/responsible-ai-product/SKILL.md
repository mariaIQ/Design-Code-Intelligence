# Responsible AI Product

Treat AI as an operational decision engine. Start from the decision being supported and the consequence of error.

Assess three connected layers: experience (intent, confidence, explanations, correction and calibrated autonomy); operations (monitoring, thresholds, fallbacks, drift, escalation and audit trails); and governance (decision rights, accountability, provenance and contestability).

For each material AI action, identify the human judgement retained, AI contribution, evidence available, failure mode and who can intervene. Separate facts, assumptions and recommendations. Do not claim compliance, safety or performance without evidence.
