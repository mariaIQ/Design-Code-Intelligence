# Case-study Storytelling

Make the strategic problem and consequential design decision the spine. Clarify setting, scale, users, constraints, personal remit, collaborators, pivotal evidence, options, decision, outcome and learning.

Distinguish individual contribution from team delivery and validated outcomes from estimates. Adapt depth to the format: concise STAR for interviews, inspectable evidence for portfolios, action–scope–outcome for CVs, and organisational value for executive presentations.

Use plain, memorable language. Avoid inflated seniority, vague claims and exhaustive method lists.
