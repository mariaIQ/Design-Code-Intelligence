# Stakeholder Briefing

Lead with the decision, outcome or risk. Translate detail into customer impact, operational efficiency, delivery confidence, risk, cost and accountability.

Use: context; evidence; why it matters; recommendation; decision required. Separate facts from interpretation and quantify only supported outcomes.

Use warm, confident, diplomatically direct British professional English. Avoid hype, generic transformation language and implied criticism. End with a specific next step, owner or decision point.
